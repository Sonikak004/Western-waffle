How it happened

The Story

We found an incredibly heavy, unnecessarily complicated waffle iron and brought it home. We figured out the dough—a yeast batter that takes 24 hours to prove and relies on thick chunks of pearl sugar that caramelize when they hit the iron.

We could have topped them with the usual generic strawberries and fake maple syrup. Instead, we grabbed what we actually like eating. Chikki. Filter coffee. Mysore pak. It’s a very old way of cooking meeting our favorite local flavors.

"It’s just hot iron and good dough. Everything else is just having fun."

— Founder, Western Waffle
